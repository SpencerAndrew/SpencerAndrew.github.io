Low Hire, Low Fire Market

A "Low Hire, Low Fire" job market is characterized by companies significantly slowing down their recruitment of new talent while simultaneously holding onto their current employees.

For job seekers, this dynamic creates a highly stagnant environment with fewer open roles and intense competition for the few available positions. This reality drastically extends the duration of the job search, negatively impacting application conversion rates and increasing the overall opportunity cost for active applicants.
